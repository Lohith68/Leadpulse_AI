All company records, individual names, email addresses, phone numbers, domain names, transaction histories, and behavioral activities contained within or generated by **LEADPULSE AI** are **100% synthetically generated** using deterministic mathematical pseudo-random algorithms (Mulberry32 PRNG) anchored to fixed seed `20261022`.

## 2. Privacy & PII Compliance
1. **Absence of Real PII:**
   - Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical addresses do not belong to real living individuals. Any resemblance to actual persons, living or deceased, is entirely coincidental.
2. **Email Domain Guard:**
   - In accordance with RFC 2606 and RFC 6761, all synthetic email addresses utilize reserved, fictitious, or demonstrative company domain names (e.g., `@example.com`, `@acmecorp.internal`, `@pulsecloud.io`). No actual emails are ever sent over SMTP to real mailboxes.
3. **No Unlawful Scraping:**
   - No data in this application was collected via web scraping, screen harvesting, or breach dumps.
4. **Regulatory Standards:**
   - The platform models compliance workflows for GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), but the underlying entities are synthetic models designed for systems evaluation.
